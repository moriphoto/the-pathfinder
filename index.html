        Map key

        Symbols are from the OpenStreetMap layer. Satellite has no path marks.

        • Yellow or orange road.
        • Brown dashes — track.
        • Pink dashes — footway.
        • Pink dots — path.
        • Green dashes — bridleway.
        • Brown line — your plotted walk.
        • Green letter — waypoint.
        • Yellow wash — CRoW access land.

        A public footpath is a legal right, not a drawing style. Check an OS map and signs on the day.
